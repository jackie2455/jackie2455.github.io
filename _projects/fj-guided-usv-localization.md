This project develops a **multimodal fusion resilient positioning system** for near-shore **unmanned surface vessels (USVs)**. It targets the robustness challenge of single-sensor localization in complex near-shore environments by fusing multiple heterogeneous sensors (GNSS, visual/inertial, millimetre-wave radar, etc.) so the vehicle can keep an accurate, low-drift estimate even when individual sources degrade or are denied.

- **Source:** Fujian Provincial Guided Project
- **Grant no.:** 2024H0016
- **Period:** 2024.05 – 2027.04
- **Role:** Principal investigator (PI), ongoing

Key work aims at high-robustness state estimation and resilient multi-source fusion for GNSS-denied or degraded marine scenes.
