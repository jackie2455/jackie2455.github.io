This project studies **key technologies for multimodal fusion resilient positioning** under **GNSS-denied** (satellite-denied) conditions. When satellite navigation is unavailable or degraded, vehicles must rely on onboard sensors (camera, inertial, lidar, encoder) to maintain an accurate and robust pose estimate. This work investigates fusion architectures and state-estimation methods that stay consistent and drift-free in such challenging scenarios.

- **Source:** University research start-up fund
- **Grant no.:** ZQ2024105
- **Period:** 2024.11 – 2026.11
- **Role:** Principal investigator (PI), ongoing

Related research: online-configurable multi-source fusion frameworks and global-velocity-constrained inertial/encoder/vision/lidar fusion localization (IEVL-Fusion).
